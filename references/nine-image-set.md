# Nine-Image Product Detail Set

Use this reference after the white-background hero and canonical copy are ready.

## Required format

Create every detail-page image on a 1:1 square canvas. Compose each frame for the square from the beginning; do not make a portrait design and crop away products, people, measurement arrows, accessories, or typography. Keep safe margins consistent across the complete set.

## Default frame sequence

Adapt wording and scenes to the product category while preserving these conversion roles:

1. **Product hero** — identify the product immediately; show key verified size, weight, or included-item facts.
2. **Primary use scene** — show the most commercially important, technically plausible real-world use.
3. **Secondary use scene** — show a distinct adjacent use or environment without broadening into an unsupported application.
4. **Core benefit** — visualize the central functional benefit through the product's real construction.
5. **Feature and structure** — show how the key mechanism, opening, frame, control, or interior works.
6. **Verified dimensions** — clean product view with deterministic measurement arrows and exact metric/imperial values.
7. **Material and detail** — macro proof of fabric, seams, hardware, closures, finish, or other verified details.
8. **Fold, pack, carry, or store** — show the actual portability or storage sequence and real carry packaging.
9. **Package contents** — accurate flat lay of included components plus verified packed dimensions and gross weight when available.

If a role does not apply, replace it with the next most important verified buying question; keep exactly nine frames.

## Campaign lock

Define once and maintain across the set: product identity and allowed states, aspect ratio, photography genre, lighting, background logic, accent colors, typefaces, natural typography zones, and the canonical copy for every frame.

## Typography

- Typeset final English deterministically after scene generation.
- Place text directly on natural negative space; do not use promotional cards, banners, badges, ribbons, frosted panels, or fake glass containers.
- Use at most three type sizes per frame and one dominant message per frame.
- Shorten copy or reposition the composition if text overlaps the product or becomes unreadable.
- Dimension numbers and conversions must come from the canonical copy record.

## Product identity

Preserve silhouette, proportions, pattern, color relationships, material behavior, seams, openings, poles, hardware, pockets, accessories, and quantity. A lifestyle scene may change camera angle or plausible state, but not product design.

## Generated-scene boundaries

Lifestyle scenes are illustrative. Avoid invented brand partnerships, named locations, documented-user claims, dangerous usage, weapons unless explicitly required, and uncontrolled readable signs or packaging. Add animals only when they establish a genuine use case.

## Final QA

- Product remains recognizable in every frame.
- No logo returns after debranding.
- No claim exceeds report evidence.
- No missing or duplicated accessory.
- All copy is exact English with no pseudo-text.
- No text is cropped, overlapped, or lost against the image.
- The nine images feel like one campaign but have distinct compositions.
- All files use stable numbered names and an exact 1:1 aspect ratio.
